GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

A few things to know before you get started.

Does VideoFetch work on every website?

It finds public MP4, HLS/M3U8 and DASH/MPD sources. DRM, live streams, private logins and sites that block automated access are not supported.

Can I paste a direct .mp4, .m3u8 or .mpd link?

Yes. Paste the direct public link. HLS/DASH segments are merged to one MP4 at your selected source quality.

Do you store my videos?

No video files are stored on disk. When you preview or download, video bytes are streamed from the public source to your device. Short-lived in-memory links expire automatically.

Why wasn't a video detected?

The page may load its playlist only after a special interaction, require login, block automated browsers, be a live stream, or use DRM. Try a direct public MP4, M3U8 or MPD link if you have one.

Will I see 480p, 720p, 1080p, 2K and 4K?

Only if the source provides them. Before you preview or download, the quality picker shows the file size in MB. Direct MP4 sizes are measured from the host; HLS/DASH sizes are marked as estimates based on playlist bitrates and duration. We never upscale a lower-resolution video and call it 4K.

Is it okay to download any video I find?

Only download videos you own or have permission to save. Please respect the source website's terms and creators' rights.
