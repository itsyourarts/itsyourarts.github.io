FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you should know about how ShadowImage2Text works with your images and your privacy.

Still curious? The OCR studio above is the best way to see it in action.
Is ShadowImage2Text really free?

Yes — fully free, with no accounts, no extraction limits and no watermarks. It’s built on the open-source Tesseract OCR engine running in your browser.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. ShadowImage2Text performs recognition locally in your browser using WebAssembly. Your image never leaves your device, which makes it safe for confidential documents, IDs and personal notes.

Does it support Hindi?

Yes. Choose हिन्दी for Devanagari text, or English + हिन्दी for documents that mix both languages. English-only mode is fastest for pure Latin text.

Which file types and sizes can I use?

JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, GIF and TIFF up to 15 MB. For the cleanest results, use sharp, well-lit images where text is straight and reasonably large.

Why is the first extraction slower?

The first run downloads the OCR core and language model (a few megabytes). They’re then cached by your browser, so later extractions start almost instantly.

How can I get the best accuracy?

Crop to the text region, keep the image straight, avoid heavy shadows or glare, and pick the correct language. Higher-resolution screenshots read noticeably better than photographed text.

Privacy

The short version: your images never leave your device.

ShadowImage2Text performs optical character recognition entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Images you load are held in memory only for the duration of your session and are never transmitted to any server.

Extracted text stays in your browser until you clear it or close the tab. Your theme preference is saved in local storage; nothing else is stored, and there are no analytics or tracking cookies on this site.

Language models and the OCR engine are fetched from public CDNs (jsDelivr and the Tesseract data host) and cached by your browser for speed.
