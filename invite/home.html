Features

Everything in one register

Writing entries, searching them, totalling the amounts and sharing the book — all four on a single neon glass page.

The complete entry, one form

Name, father's name, phone, address, amount, cash/UPI/cheque, side (bride or groom) and date — all captured together.

  • Phone & address optional
  • Today's date auto-filled
  • Entry lands in the register instantly
⇩

7 export formats

The whole book in one click as PDF, XML, JSON, CSV, PNG, HTML or Markdown — or everything together in a single ZIP.

  • Pick your columns
  • Filter by date range
  • “Export all” as ZIP
⌁

Neon glass design

Real glassmorphism — layered blur, gradient borders, animated glow and falling rose petals. Reports stay beautiful in print.

  • Perfect on mobile too
  • Dark-first theme
  • Smooth animations

Family data stays private

No phone number or address is ever uploaded. Everything lives in your browser's localStorage and nowhere else.

  • No account needed
  • Works without internet
  • One-click data wipe
Σ

Totals that do themselves

Total shagun, largest gift, average, and a cash-versus-online breakdown — all updating live as you write.

  • Total + average
  • Highest / lowest entry
  • Method-wise and side-wise split

Find anyone instantly

“Who was that Sharma ji?” — search by name, father's name, phone or locality, and filter by side, method or date.

  • Instant search
  • Side + method filters
  • Date range filter
Workflow

The book is ready in 3 steps

  1. 01

    Write

    Fill in the guest's name, father's name, phone, address and the amount they gave — then press “Add entry”.

  2. 02

    Review

    Search the register, filter by side or date. Totals and breakups keep updating on their own.

  3. 03

    Export

    PDF or PNG for the family group, CSV for the accounts, XML/JSON for backup — or everything in one ZIP.
