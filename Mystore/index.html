Your next app, in 3 steps.

  1. Pick an app and tap Download.
  2. Keep this page open while the APK is fetched. Your browser then saves the file or asks where to save it.
  3. Open the APK on Android. Allow installation from your browser only if you trust the app.

This independent store is not Google Play. App icons are extracted from APK launcher resources when supported. Unsupported icons use a placeholder. First-time extraction downloads APK data (up to 40 MB per app); extracted icons are cached in your browser. Version names, ratings and security claims are not invented.

Direct-link downloads are managed by your browser, so their progress cannot be tracked here.
